## 1. Architectural Vision & "Sovereign Civic" Design System (`america.gov` + Google Material Design)

The Digital Republic of Novatlantis adopts the **Sovereign Civic** design system, directly inspired by **[america.gov](https://america.gov/)** and **Google Material Design (`@mui/material` v6)** navigation guidelines, engineered to reflect **institutional austerity, algorithmic transparency, and continuous public service for the citizenry**.

### 1.1 Visual Guidelines, Tokens & Material Design Navigation
- **Light, Austere Surfaces:**
  - Primary Background (`surface`): `#FCFBF9` (Warm Cream Editorial) / `#F7F9FC`
  - Institutional Cards (`surface-container-lowest`): `#FFFFFF` (Pure White)
  - Secondary Container (`surface-container-low`): `#F2F4F7`
  - Structural Borders (`civic-border` / `outline-variant`): `1px solid #E5E4DC` / `#DCE3EC`
- **State Authority Colors:**
  - Sovereign Header (`primary-container` / `sovereign-navy`): `#0A2240` / `#002046`
  - Administrative Blue (`secondary`): `#0061A5`
  - Verified Consensus Teal (`tertiary`): `#006B5B`
  - Civil 911 Emergency Red (`error`): `#991B1B` / `#BA1A1A`
- **Official Typography:**
  - Editorial State Headings: `Merriweather` (`700`, `900`)
  - Official Labels & Body Copy: `Public Sans` / `Inter` (`400`, `600`, `700`, `800`) with `tabular-nums`
  - Cryptographic Identifiers, NIDs & Hashes: `JetBrains Mono`
- **Google Material Design Persistent Navigation Drawer Pattern (Sidebar without Overlay):**
  - **Full-Page Sovereign Profile (`fullScreen`) with Side-by-Side Sidebar:** Clicking the user profile (`TopNavUserWidget`) opens a **Full-Page View (`fullScreen`)** featuring a **persistent side Navigation Drawer (Sidebar) (`<Box component="aside">`)** placed alongside the main content area (`flex: 1`), eliminating small popup overlays.
  - **Modules Menu as Persistent Navigation Drawer (Sidebar):** Across all 3 portals (`landing-portal`, `citizen-portal`, and `gov-backstage`), the Hamburger button (`☰`) toggles a **persistent side Navigation Drawer (Sidebar)** that sits side-by-side with `<main>` in a horizontal flex layout, following Google Material Design rather than opening a modal overlay drawer.

### 2.1 Transactional (AlloyDB) & Medallion Analytical Architecture (GDP on Google Cloud `novatlantis`)
1. **Sovereign Transactional Layer (AlloyDB for PostgreSQL):**

## 3. Access Governance: Unified Login & Identity 360 Application

### 3.1 Single Citizen / Public Servant Login Principle
There are no separate accounts for citizens and public servants. Every individual authenticates using their **NID (`NID-AAA-BBBB-CCCC-D`)** or **Civic Email**.
At login (`POST /api/auth/login` or `POST /api/v1/auth/login`), the engine queries the **Identity 360** role table:
1. If the citizen holds an active appointment, they receive their administrative role (`effective_role_code`) and ministerial scopes in the **Government Backstage**.
2. **Immediate Revocation:** As soon as permission is revoked in the **Identity 360** application (`POST /api/iam360/revoke`), `iam_role` reverts immediately to `CITIZEN_COMMON`. The user instantaneously becomes a **Common Citizen** again and loses access to administrative environments.

## 4. Functional Documentation of the 3 Portals & Separation of 311 and 911 Modules

### 4.1 National Main Portal (`apps/landing-portal`)
- **Persistent Navigation Drawer (Sidebar `☰`):** Opens alongside the main page content (Google Material Design pattern), providing direct links to citizen modules and administrative environments.
- **Public National AI Concierge:** Allows any visitor to ask public questions about State services in Portuguese, Spanish, or English without signing in.
- **7 Essential Sovereign Service Cards:** Includes dedicated separate cards for **311 Urban Services** (`?tab=urban_311`) and **911 Emergency (Tactical & Medical SOS)** (`?tab=emergency_911`).

### 4.2 Citizen Portal (`apps/citizen-portal`) — 7 Independent Modules with Persistent Sidebar
1. **1. Sovereign NID Wallet & NIST Biometrics (`identity`):** Mod-11 credential, `Ed25519` public key, NIST facial confidence score, and 48h Zero-Trust access audit trail.
2. **2. Family Graph & Sovereign Address (`family_address`):** Strictly Read-Only view of the civil family graph (`rel_family_graph`) and real-time residence update.
3. **3. HL7 Healthcare & 24/7 Telemedicine (`health`):** National HL7 FHIR Electronic Health Record and AI-assisted teleconsultations with digital ICP prescriptions.
4. **4. Education & School Grades (`education`):** Subject report card (`Mathematics`, `Sciences`, `AI & Robotics`, `Languages`) and attendance synchronized with the teacher Backstage.
5. **5. 311 Urban Services (`urban_311`):** Dedicated module exclusively for opening and tracking urban maintenance tickets (`ops_311_tickets`).
6. **6. 911 Emergency — Tactical & Medical SOS (`emergency_911`):** Dedicated high-priority module exclusively for immediate 911 emergency dispatch (Mobile ICU, Civil Defense, and Coast Guard) with live ETA tracking (`ops_911_dispatches`).
7. **7. Economy, 45s Company & ICAO Passport (`treasury`):** Instantaneous 45-second company incorporation (`GovBiz 45s`) and ICAO Doc 9303 Biometric Passport issuance.

### 4.3 Government Backstage (`apps/gov-backstage`) — 7 Administrative Environments with Persistent Sidebar
1. **1. Prime Minister (`Joao Thiago Poço - JT`) & Secretary-General Cabinet (`pm_cabinet`):** Executive command of the nation and real-time KPIs from the Government Data Fabric (100,000 citizens).
2. **2. Identity 360 Application (`iam360`):** Instantaneous granting and revocation of RBAC/ABAC administrative roles with immediate reversion to `CITIZEN_COMMON`.
3. **3. Healthcare, Hospital & Doctor Management (`health_mgmt`):** Real-time monitoring of the 6 national hospitals and telemedicine queue.
4. **4. Education, School, Exam & Grade Management (`edu_mgmt`):** Teacher gradebook for subject grade entry and national exam publication.
5. **5. 311 Urban Maintenance Command (`ops_311`):** Dedicated queue exclusively for handling and resolving citizen 311 urban maintenance tickets (`POST /api/services/311/resolve`).
6. **6. 911 Emergency Dispatch Command (`ops_911`):** Dedicated console exclusively for real-time monitoring of tactical and medical 911 dispatches (`ops_911_dispatches`).
7. **7. Justice, Treasury, AlloyDB & GDP 100k (`justice_datalake`):** Live SQL explorer across all 100,000 citizens in AlloyDB (`10.223.28.2:5432`) and BigQuery GDP (`novatlantis_gdp_dwh_cur_bq_0`).

## 5. Technical Reference of REST APIs

| Method | Endpoint | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `GET` | `/api/health` | Operational status of AlloyDB, SQLite WAL (100k citizens), and Government Data Platform datasets on GCP. |
| `POST` | `/api/v1/auth/login` | Authenticates by NID or Email with password verification, brute-force protection, and JWT token (`FIRST_LOGIN_REQUIRED` or `FULL_ACCESS`). |
| `POST` | `/api/v1/auth/first-login-password` | Mandatory change of initial postal password upon first login and issuance of a full sovereign session. |
| `GET` | `/api/v1/profile/me` | Returns the authenticated citizen's complete profile, including official photo (`photo_url`), native language (`native_language`), and Read-Only family graph (`family_links`). |
| `PUT` | `/api/v1/profile/me` | Persists profile updates (`photo_url` compressed in Base64, `social_name`, `preferred_contact`, `accessibility_needs`, `native_language`, `street_address`, `district`) via `UPSERT` into `citizen_profiles` and `dim_citizens` in **AlloyDB** and SQLite WAL. |
| `GET` | `/api/gdf/search?q=&limit=` | SQL search over the 100,000 citizens filtered by name, NID, profession, or district. |
| `GET` | `/api/iam360/roles` | Lists all active permissions in the Identity 360 application. |
| `POST` | `/api/iam360/grant` | Grants Backstage administrative permission in Identity 360. |
| `POST` | `/api/iam360/revoke` | Revokes administrative permission and immediately reverts the user to `CITIZEN_COMMON`. |
| `POST` | `/api/services/311` | Registers a new ticket in the dedicated **311 Urban Services** module (`ops_311_tickets`). |
| `POST` | `/api/services/311/resolve` | Resolves a 311 ticket by a public servant in the **311 Urban Maintenance Command**. |
| `POST` | `/api/services/911` | Triggers an immediate dispatch in the dedicated **911 Emergency** module (`ops_911_dispatches`). |
| `POST` | `/api/services/company` | Incorporates an autonomous company in 45 seconds linked to the citizen's NID. |
| `POST` | `/api/services/passport` | Issues or revalidates an ICAO Doc 9303 Biometric Passport. |
